Frequently asked questions

Do I need to set up a Mexican entity from day one?

Not always. Many companies validate the market with pilots or an employer of record, and incorporate once they have customers. We decide this in the assessment.

Do you work with companies from any country?

Yes. We work with companies from Latin America, the United States, Europe and Asia, in English or Spanish.

How is the service priced?

By phase: a fixed fee for the assessment and the plan, and a monthly retainer during setup and supported operation.

What happens when the engagement ends?

Your local team takes over with documented processes. If you prefer, we stay on as an advisory board.
