For any matter related to this notice, write to angelo@aterrizajemexico.com or call +52 56 1033 0956.

2. Personal data we collect

We collect the data you give us directly through this site’s contact form, by email, by phone or on WhatsApp:

  • Identification data: name.
  • Contact data: email address and, where applicable, phone number.
  • Business data: the company you work for, its home country and any information you share about your plans in Mexico.

We do not request sensitive personal data. Please do not include it in your messages.

3. Purposes of processing

Primary purposes, needed to handle your request:

  • Replying to your message and questions.
  • Scheduling and conducting the initial assessment.
  • Preparing and sending you service proposals.
  • Managing any business relationship that results.

Secondary purposes, not needed to handle your request:

  • Occasionally sending you information about our services, events and content on entering the Mexican market.

If you do not want your data used for the secondary purpose, email angelo@aterrizajemexico.com with the subject “I do not wish to receive information”. Opting out will not affect how we handle your request.

4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell or assign your personal data. We do not make any transfers of your data to third parties that would require your consent.

To run this site and communicate with you, we rely on service providers that process data only on our behalf and under our instructions, some of them located outside Mexico:

  • FormSubmit: delivers contact form messages to our email.
  • Google Workspace: email.
  • Railway: website hosting.

We may disclose your data to competent authorities when required by law or by a duly founded order.

5. Your ARCO rights and withdrawal of consent

You have the right to access your personal data, rectify or update it, cancel it and object to its processing for specific purposes (known in Mexico as ARCO rights). You may also withdraw your consent at any time, to the extent permitted by law.

To exercise these rights, send a request to angelo@aterrizajemexico.com including:

  • Your name and an email address where we can send our reply.
  • A document proving your identity or, where applicable, that of your legal representative.
  • A clear description of the data and the right you wish to exercise.
  • Any information that helps us locate your data.

We will reply by email within the time limits set by law and, if your request is valid, carry it out within those same limits.

6. Data retention

We keep your data only as long as needed for the purposes described and for any applicable legal obligations. After that, we delete it securely.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not use advertising cookies or tracking tools. To display its fonts, your browser connects to Google Fonts, which may send Google technical data such as your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice due to legal changes or changes in our services. Any update will be posted on this page with its date.

9. Authority

If you believe your right to personal data protection has been violated, you may contact the competent Mexican authority, currently the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno.
